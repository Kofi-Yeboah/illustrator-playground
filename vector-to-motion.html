Vector to Motion

Learn vector editing properly in Illustrator first, carry it into Affinity and Figma, then bring your own drawings to life in Cavalry and After Effects. Every app has two courses: a tools tour with one small task per tool, then a project ladder built from pins you saved.

Statics before motion

Motion design is vector shapes changing over time. If you can build it cleanly, you can animate it.

Your saves are the syllabus

Every project links to a pin you saved, graded by the tools it needs, so saving turns into making.

Reuse your own files

Projects marked uses your file animate something you already drew in an earlier stage.

A good week about 5 hours

  1. Two short sessions: 3 to 4 tools from the tools course, one micro-task each.
  2. One long session: one project from the ladder. Rebuild the pin, then make a version with your own word, colours or subject.
  3. Post it: put the rebuild next to the original. Seeing the gap is the lesson.
  4. Move up a rung after 2 projects feel easy. Don't skip Illustrator's beginner rung, even if it feels slow.
